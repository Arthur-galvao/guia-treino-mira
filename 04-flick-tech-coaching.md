# Flick Tech, Aim Coaching by thrFPS

KovaaK's Share Code: `KovaaKsSwitchingSeagreenHeaven`

## Deconstructing Bad Habits and the Anatomy of a Flick

The purpose of this playlist is to dismantle bad habits in your flick mechanics while reinforcing the primary goal: establishing correct flick anatomy, embedding healthy physical habits, preventing rushed shots, and maintaining muscular tension strictly under control.

You cannot effectively build advanced fundamentals such as target reading if rushing shots remains an automatic habit in your aiming repertoire. The focus here is mastering the fundamental "pyramid" motion before advancing to more sophisticated scenarios.

Objective: develop flick anatomy. Context: Flick Tech. Scope: fundamentals.

## Playlist Tasks Breakdown

### 1. WALLHACK - VBRClick Easy

Begins the playlist with a foundational scenario reinforcing core flick mechanics: explosive initial motion, controlled tension curve, smooth micro-adjustments, and zero rushed clicking on autopilot.

Because the wall area is wide, an explosive initial movement initiated from the forearm, followed by smooth micro-corrections using the wrist and fingers, becomes a natural gameplay requirement due to target distribution. Target vibration further demands strict target visual confirmation. Neglecting these fundamentals here will result in downstream inconsistency.

### 2. 1w2ts Pasu Perfected

With baseline mechanics warmed up, this task addresses common failures from the previous exercise: inconsistent pacing and poor tension management.

The design is straightforward. An explosive initial movement (though less aggressive than before, as target displacement is larger, requiring extended micro-adjustments). 1w2ts is one of the most essential static-adjacent variants in aim training, compelling you to maintain clean linear pathing and continuous fluidity.

### 3. 1w4t_vbr Raspberry

Flicks here are smaller on average, but that does not make the task easier. The reload dynamic departs from standard scenarios: there is no bullet replenishment on kill, and every hit adds 0.125 seconds of task duration.

Missing a shot forces an agonizing ~3-second reload animation. This shatters your pathing, breaks your pacing, tanks your score, and tests your mental composure.

### 4. Sevenshot SV

The first task devoid of target vibration or motion, which is precisely where the challenge lies. Previous tasks provided immediate visual negative feedback when missing moving targets. Here, abundant targets create the false impression of simplicity, tempting you to abandon target confirmation and tension regulation in favor of mindless click spamming.

That is precisely the trap. Missing severely damages your score due to accuracy weighting, and targets sit in close proximity: over-tensing here compromises execution fluidity and injects systemic inconsistency into your broader mechanics.

### 5. WALLHACK - VBRClick Hard (GRIND)

Grind this scenario until you achieve a minimum of 1000 points.
