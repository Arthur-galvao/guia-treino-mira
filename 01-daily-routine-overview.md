# Daily Routine Overview

This is the exact sequence to follow during every training session. Do not skip steps: each stage prepares your mechanics for the next.

## 1. Warm-up and Technical Foundation (Daily)

Play the **Flick Tech** playlist by thrFPS in KovaaK's. Share code: `KovaaKsSwitchingSeagreenHeaven`.

Only advance to the next task after hitting your daily **Threshold** on the current task. (What a Threshold is and how to calculate it is covered in Part 2.)

## 2. Weakness Development (Main Training)

Play the **Viscose Benchmark** by robinwobin.

Check your tracker and identify your lowest-performing scenarios. Grind those specific scenarios until they rank up, rather than running through the entire benchmark every session. The same Threshold rule applies here: never skip a task without hitting your target value for the day.

## 3. Progress Tracking

Log your scores and thresholds using the Escalate format. The full logging syntax and examples are detailed in Part 5.

## 4. In-Game Movement and Mechanics Training

Follow the Valorant movement training plan developed by KoreyVAL, utilizing The Range and Deathmatch. Detailed drills and level criteria are covered in Part 6.
