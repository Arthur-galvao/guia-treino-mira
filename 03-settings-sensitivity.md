## Benchmark Progression and Difficulty Tiers

If you are a beginner, start with the "Viscose Entry Benchmarks". For intermediate or advanced players, start directly on "Viscose Benchmarks S2". Track your progression using the Raw Input Benchmark Web Tracker.

When should you advance in difficulty? Only after achieving the second-highest rank and completing the third-highest rank on your current tier. Incorporate harder scenarios only when you are at least 4 ranks below maximum on the easier tier.

- Viscose Benchmark Overview: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KwCzQmSx-p0

## What is cm/360?

cm/360 measures the physical distance in centimeters required to move your mouse to complete a 360-degree rotation in-game. Standardizing your sensitivity scale in KovaaK's to cm/360 allows you to communicate and adjust mouse sensitivity with complete precision across all games and titles.

- Sensitivity & cm/360 Converter: https://kovaaks.com/kovaaks/cm360-converter

## Sensitivity by Scenario Category

Varying sensitivity according to scenario type develops broader mouse control and engages diverse muscle groups in a balanced manner, preventing over-reliance on a single movement motor pattern.
